FAQ

Common questions

Is this a debt collection agency?
No. CashQueue helps you send professional payment reminders from your own business email. You approve messages and maintain the customer relationship.
Will it send emails without my approval?
Not at first. You review and click send. Auto-send on trusted sequences is optional once you're comfortable with the tone.
How is this different from Chaser or Upflow?
Same category — we're built AI-first for SMBs: smarter prioritization ("who pays if I nudge today"), reply-aware promise tracking, and tone that adapts per customer.
Who is this for?
B2B businesses on QuickBooks or Xero with $50k+ overdue AR — agencies, distributors, trades, professional services. If someone still sends reminder emails manually, this is for you.
